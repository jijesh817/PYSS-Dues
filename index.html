How due is worked out: dues start from the first year a member paid; if they have not paid yet, from their joining year. The first two years are 2,400 each, then 2,000 per year, up to 2026. Due = expected − total paid so far. Payments are counted as a total, so an extra payment in one year offsets a short one in another.

New payments are recorded by the treasurer in the PYSS payments Google Sheet and appear here when you open or refresh the app.
